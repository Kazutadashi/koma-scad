# Code of conduct

KomaSCAD is a small project of people who like shogi, 3D printing, or both.
We want it to be a friendly place for beginners and experts alike.

## What we expect

- Be kind and patient, especially with people who are new to printing, to
  programming, or to shogi. Every expert was a beginner once.
- Assume good intent. Ask before you criticize, and criticize the work, not
  the person.
- Give credit: name the people whose prints, ideas and code you build on.
- Respect people's time: search the issues first, and give the details the
  forms ask for.

## What is not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks.
- Discriminatory jokes or language about anyone's background, identity,
  nationality, language or ability.
- Posting other people's private information.
- Sustained disruption of discussions.

## What happens

The maintainers may edit or remove comments, close issues, or block people
who do not follow this code, in any space of the project.

To report a problem privately, contact the maintainer,
[@Kazutadashi](https://github.com/Kazutadashi), through the contact details on
their GitHub profile.
