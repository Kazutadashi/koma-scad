# Komadai (駒台): the piece stand

The komadai is the small stand next to the board. Each player puts the pieces
that they capture on their komadai, so that the opponent can see them.

| Path | Contents |
| --- | --- |
| `komadai.scad` | The komadai model. Open it in OpenSCAD. |

The [komadai guide](../docs/komadai.md) tells you how to make one, and gives
every setting.
