From a Wikipedia diagram

Wikipedia's movement diagrams use the same grid: the piece in the middle, forward up. Copy them square by square, using these marks.

Wikipedia Meaning Use
○ Steps to this square; several in a row are a limited range Move
│ ─ ╲ ╱ Ranges along the line Slide, on the first square of the line
╳ ┼ May turn 90° at this square Hook, on the first square of the line
☆ Jumps to this square Jump
○ │ ─ ╲ ╱ Steps or ranges on after a ☆ Move or Slide, beyond the jump on the same line
3 Jumps up to 3 squares, then may range (Wikipedia notes the meaning is uncertain) Jump on squares 1–3, then Slide on square 4
☆ Jumps there, or reaches it in two steps Jump; a lion with every square within two becomes a frame
